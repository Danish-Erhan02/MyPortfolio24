Response at the probe frequency

Wave speed and length now follow the probe frequency (compressed to a viewable range); amplitude is the response to a 1 m wave.
How the curves are built. Each mode is a forced mass–spring–damper: RAO = E(ω)·C/√[(C−I·ω²)²+(Bω)²], where E(ω)=e^(−kT)·|sinc| is the wave pressure & length averaging. Restoring C, virtual inertia I, and damping B come from box geometry.
Why the wiggles. The |sinc(kL/2)| (heave/pitch) and |sinc(kB/2)| (roll) terms create cancellation dips where wave crests and troughs balance along the hull — a real effect for box-shaped hulls.
